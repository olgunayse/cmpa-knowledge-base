# Itineraries

One of the biggest challenges when planning a trip is deciding how to fit everything into the time you have. While researching Europe, I found myself constantly asking questions like how many cities were realistic in one week or whether it was better to spend more time in fewer places. There isn't one perfect itinerary because every traveler has different interests, but having a general plan makes the trip much easier to organize.

I created this section to bring together destination ideas into practical travel plans. These itineraries aren't meant to be followed exactly. Instead, they can serve as a starting point that travelers can adjust based on their schedule, budget, and personal interests.
