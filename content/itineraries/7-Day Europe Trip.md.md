# 7-Day Europe Trip

## Overview

If I had one week to travel through Europe, I wouldn't try to visit as many countries as possible just to say I had been there. After researching different destinations, I realized that constantly moving between cities can take away from the experience. I would rather spend enough time in each place to actually explore it than rush through a long checklist of attractions.

This itinerary combines several destinations from this knowledge base while keeping travel realistic. It focuses on experiencing different landscapes, cultures, and cities without making every day feel like a race.

## Day 1 – Switzerland

Start the trip in Switzerland by exploring Zurich before continuing toward the Alps. Spending the first day adjusting to the new time zone while walking through the city and along the lake seems like a relaxing way to begin the trip.

## Day 2 – Swiss Alps

Travel to Interlaken or Lauterbrunnen to experience the mountains, hiking trails, and scenic viewpoints. This would probably be the day I'd spend taking the most photographs.

## Day 3 – Dolomites

Continue into northern Italy and explore the Dolomites. Whether hiking, driving through mountain passes, or simply enjoying the scenery, this region seems worth slowing down and appreciating.

## Day 4 – Venice

Spend a day exploring Venice before continuing farther south. The canals, bridges, and historic architecture make it one of the most recognizable cities in Europe.

## Day 5 – Dubrovnik

Fly to Dubrovnik and spend the afternoon exploring the Old Town and walking along the city walls overlooking the Adriatic Sea.

## Day 6 – Santorini

Continue to Santorini and enjoy the island's villages, beaches, and famous sunset views. After several busy travel days, this would be a nice place to slow the pace a little.

## Day 7 – Return Home

Spend the final morning exploring, shopping, or relaxing before returning home. Leaving a little extra time before departure also helps reduce the stress that often comes with travel days.

## Final Thoughts

If I were actually planning this trip, I would probably make changes depending on flight prices, transportation schedules, and how much time I wanted to spend in each destination. That's one thing I learned while working on this project: the best itinerary is usually the one that stays flexible.
