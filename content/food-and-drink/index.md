# Food and Drink

Whenever I look into a new destination, one of the first things I become curious about is the food. Every country has dishes that reflect its history, culture, and local ingredients, and trying those foods seems like one of the easiest ways to experience a place beyond its famous landmarks. I don't think a trip is only about checking attractions off a list—it is also about experiencing everyday life, and food is a big part of that.

This section highlights some of the traditional foods that make each destination unique. Rather than trying to cover everything, I wanted to focus on meals and dining experiences that travelers are most likely to encounter while exploring Europe.
