# Traditional Foods

## Overview

One thing I noticed while researching Europe is that every destination has foods that people immediately associate with it. Before starting this project, I already knew about a few famous dishes like pizza and fondue, but I was surprised by how much regional variety exists even within the same country. Learning about local cuisine made each destination feel more unique and helped me appreciate how food reflects a country's history and traditions.

## Switzerland

Switzerland is well known for dishes like cheese fondue, raclette, and rösti. The country is also famous for its chocolate, which is often recommended as something every visitor should try.

## Italy

Italian cuisine goes far beyond pizza and pasta. Different regions specialize in different recipes, making it a destination where local food is just as much of an attraction as the scenery.

## Greece

Greek food often features fresh vegetables, olive oil, seafood, grilled meats, and cheeses. Meals are usually simple, fresh, and designed to be shared with others.

## Croatia

Croatia's coastal regions are known for fresh seafood, while inland areas offer grilled meats, stews, and pastries. The variety changes depending on where you are in the country.

## What I Learned

Researching traditional foods reminded me that trying local cuisine is part of learning about a destination. Even if I don't enjoy every dish, I think being willing to try something new is one of the most rewarding parts of traveling.
