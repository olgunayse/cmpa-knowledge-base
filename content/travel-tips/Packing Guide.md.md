# Packing Guide

## Overview

Packing seems simple until you start thinking about everything you might need while traveling through multiple destinations. While researching this project, I noticed that many experienced travelers recommended bringing less than you think you'll need. Moving between airports, train stations, and hotels becomes much easier when your luggage is manageable.

Instead of packing for every possible situation, I would rather focus on versatile clothing and essential items that can be used throughout the trip.

## What I Would Pack

If I were planning a trip through Europe, these would probably be the first things on my packing list.

- Passport and travel documents
- Phone charger and power adapter
- Comfortable walking shoes
- Weather-appropriate clothing
- Prescription medications
- Portable charger
- Reusable water bottle

## Things I Learned

One piece of advice that appeared repeatedly during my research was to check the weather for every destination before packing. Europe has many different climates, and temperatures can vary significantly depending on the country and the season. Packing clothes that can be layered seems much more practical than bringing bulky items that only work in one type of weather.

I also think leaving extra space in your suitcase is a good idea. Whether it's souvenirs or simply making repacking easier, having a little extra room seems worth it.
