# Destinations

When I first started thinking about this project, I knew I wanted it to focus on places that genuinely interested me instead of trying to cover every country in Europe. The destinations in this section are all places that stood out to me for different reasons. Some are known for incredible natural scenery, while others are famous for their history, architecture, or culture. Together, they represent the kind of trip I would love to take.

While researching these locations, I realized that every destination offers something completely different. Switzerland is known for its mountains and lakes, the Dolomites for outdoor adventures, Dubrovnik for its historic streets, Santorini for its island views, and the Lofoten Islands for their dramatic landscapes. Comparing them made me appreciate how diverse Europe really is.
