# About

My name is Ayse, and I am a Computing Applications student at Texas Tech University. Outside of school, I enjoy traveling, photography, and learning about technology. Traveling has become one of my favorite hobbies because it allows me to experience new cultures, meet different people, and see places that are completely different from what I'm used to. A road trip through several European countries sparked my interest in European travel and inspired me to choose this topic for my knowledge base.

As I worked on this project, I realized that planning a trip involves much more than simply choosing a destination. Transportation, accommodations, budgeting, food, and practical travel tips all play an important role in creating a successful travel experience. Instead of keeping my research scattered across different websites, I wanted to organize everything into one place that would be easy to navigate and continue building over time.

Although I haven't visited every destination included in this knowledge base, each one was chosen because it genuinely interests me. Researching these places gave me a greater appreciation for the diversity of Europe, from mountain landscapes and historic cities to coastal villages and unique local traditions. I hope this knowledge base can serve as a useful resource for anyone who is planning a trip through Europe or simply wants to learn more about some of its most interesting destinations.
