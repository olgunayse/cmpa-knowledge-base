# Budgeting

One thing I realized while researching different destinations is that there isn't a single answer to the question, "How much does a trip to Europe cost?" The budget depends on where you go, when you travel, how you get around, and what kind of experience you want. Someone staying in hostels and using public transportation will have a completely different budget than someone booking hotels and guided tours.

I wanted this section to focus less on exact numbers and more on the factors that influence travel costs. Having a realistic budget before leaving makes it much easier to enjoy the trip without constantly worrying about money or unexpected expenses.
