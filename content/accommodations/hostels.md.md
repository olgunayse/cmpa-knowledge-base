# Hostels

## Overview

Before planning this project, I mostly associated hostels with backpackers and students. After learning more about them, I realized they're much more diverse than I expected. Many offer private rooms in addition to shared dormitories, making them a practical option for travelers who want to save money without giving up comfort completely.

Hostels also seem to create a different kind of travel experience. Instead of simply having a place to sleep, many encourage travelers to meet each other through shared kitchens, lounges, and organized activities.

## Why Someone Might Choose a Hostel

For travelers on a tighter budget, staying in a hostel can free up money for experiences like museums, tours, or local restaurants. They're also a good option for solo travelers who want opportunities to meet people from different countries.

That said, hostels probably aren't the best choice for everyone. Travelers looking for more privacy or a quieter environment may prefer a hotel instead.

## Things I Would Check Before Booking

While researching hostels, these were the factors that seemed most important.

- Recent guest reviews.
- Secure lockers for personal belongings.
- Location near public transportation.
- Whether private rooms are available.
- Shared kitchen facilities.
