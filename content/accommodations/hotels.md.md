# Hotels

## Overview

Before researching this project, I mostly thought of hotels as the default place to stay while traveling. The more I looked into different destinations across Europe, the more I realized that every hotel offers a different experience. Some focus on luxury and amenities, while others simply provide a clean, comfortable place to sleep after a full day of exploring.

If I were planning a trip through several countries, I think hotels would make the most sense in larger cities or after long travel days. Having a private room, a comfortable bed, and someone available at the front desk would probably make moving between destinations much less stressful.

## What I Look For

After comparing hotels in different countries, a few things stood out as being more important than everything else.

- A location close to public transportation or the city center.
- Recent reviews instead of only looking at the overall rating.
- Flexible cancellation policies in case travel plans change.
- Breakfast included, especially for busy sightseeing days.

Expensive amenities like fitness centers or large pools look impressive in photos, but they probably wouldn't influence my decision very much. I'd rather stay somewhere that's convenient than somewhere with facilities I may never use.

## Booking Tips

One thing I noticed while researching accommodations is that prices can change quite a bit depending on when you book and which website you use. Comparing a few different booking sites seems worthwhile, especially during the busy travel season.

I'd also spend a little extra time looking at the hotel's location on a map. Saving twenty dollars on a room isn't always worth it if it means spending an extra hour commuting every day.
