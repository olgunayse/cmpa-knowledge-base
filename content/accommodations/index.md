# Accommodations

When I first started researching Europe, I thought finding a place to stay would be one of the easiest parts of planning a trip. Instead, it quickly became one of the biggest decisions. Every destination had hundreds of hotels, hostels, apartments, and guesthouses, all with different prices, locations, and reviews. It made me realize that where you stay can shape your entire experience just as much as the places you visit.

For this knowledge base, I wanted to include information that would actually help someone compare different accommodation options instead of simply listing them. The pages in this section explain what makes hotels and hostels different, what I noticed while researching them, and the factors I would consider before booking.
