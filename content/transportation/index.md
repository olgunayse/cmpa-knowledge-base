# Transportation

One thing that stood out to me while researching Europe was how connected everything seemed to be. Unlike road trips where driving is often the only practical option, many European destinations can be reached by train, bus, ferry, or a short flight. Deciding how to get from one place to another can be just as important as deciding where to go.

I created this section to organize what I learned about traveling between destinations. Understanding the different transportation options makes it easier to plan realistic itineraries, estimate travel costs, and avoid unnecessary stress while moving from one country to the next.
